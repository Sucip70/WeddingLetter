# Sekar Keraton — Raster-Only Artwork Rule

All decorative artwork for Sekar Keraton must be created as artist-quality raster artwork.

## Required production formats

- Transparent PNG master for overlays and movable artwork.
- Quality-90 WebP companion for web delivery.
- Opaque PNG or WebP for paper backgrounds.
- Editable layered raster source when available, such as PSD or Krita.

## Prohibited workflow

- Do not generate, trace, reconstruct, or approximate batik, kayon, gate artwork, frames, dividers, or other decoration with SVG.
- Do not use programmatic vector geometry as the source of production or preview artwork.
- Do not simplify approved cultural artwork into repeated geometric symbols for engineering convenience.

The approved Sogan Batik concept board remains the visual source of truth. Implementation assets must preserve its painterly detail, organic variation, remukan texture, cultural character, and visual quality.
