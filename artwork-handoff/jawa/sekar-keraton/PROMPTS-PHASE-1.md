# Sekar Keraton Phase 1 prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation. Use case: `stylized-concept`.

Three text-free, 1024×1536 material foundations were generated:

- **Ivory Keraton:** warm ivory handmade paper, restrained sogan and muted antique-gold edge detail, calm central field.
- **Maroon Pendopo:** deep-maroon framing, ivory center, court-architectural material cues without a literal building.
- **Sogan Batik:** warm sogan paper, narrow diagonal parang-inspired edge rhythm, quiet central field.

All prompts explicitly excluded people, wayang characters, palace landscapes, flowers, bottom bouquets, full-page batik, Balinese ornament, bright yellow metal, tourism-poster styling, typography, logos, and watermarks.

Generated foundations were normalized locally, then combined with deterministic preview typography and restrained motif geometry. This prevents ambiguous generated ornament from becoming the final cultural vocabulary.
