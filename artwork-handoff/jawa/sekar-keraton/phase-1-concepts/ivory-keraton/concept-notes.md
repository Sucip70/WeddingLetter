# Ivory Keraton

Visual revision: `phase-1-concept-r1` (not a database artwork version)

## Direction

Hairline keraton framing; one small kayon; kawung only as divider. Quietest and most versatile.

## Typography

Suggested pairing: **Cormorant Garamond + Inter**. Review previews use system fallbacks only; engineering should load the approved typefaces after concept selection.

## Palette

- Ivory: `#f3ead8`
- Sogan: `#76502f`
- Deep maroon: `#651f29`
- Antique gold: `#aa8244`
- Soft charcoal: `#342e29`

## Motif discipline

- Use one small kayon and a hairline keraton frame.
- Reserve kawung for dividers.
- Do not stack the frame with full parang bands.

## Phase boundary

These files are review previews only. They are not final production covers, transparent gate layers, or reusable content assets. Phase 2 must wait for explicit concept approval.
