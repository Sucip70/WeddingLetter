# Sogan Batik

Visual revision: `phase-1-concept-r1` (not a database artwork version)

## Direction

Parang stays at diagonal edges; kawung becomes punctuation. Strongest traditional editorial voice.

## Typography

Suggested pairing: **Libre Baskerville + Source Sans 3**. Review previews use system fallbacks only; engineering should load the approved typefaces after concept selection.

## Palette

- Ivory: `#ead6ad`
- Sogan: `#74401f`
- Deep maroon: `#68252a`
- Antique gold: `#aa7d38`
- Soft charcoal: `#3a291e`

## Motif discipline

- Keep parang in narrow diagonal edge bands.
- Use kawung only as punctuation or a divider.
- Never place pattern behind names, guest text, or CTA.

## Phase boundary

These files are review previews only. They are not final production covers, transparent gate layers, or reusable content assets. Phase 2 must wait for explicit concept approval.
