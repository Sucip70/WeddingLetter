# Maroon Pendopo

Visual revision: `phase-1-concept-r1` (not a database artwork version)

## Direction

Abstract pendopo roof line and gebyok posts frame an ivory field. Architectural, never scenic.

## Typography

Suggested pairing: **EB Garamond + Manrope**. Review previews use system fallbacks only; engineering should load the approved typefaces after concept selection.

## Palette

- Ivory: `#f4ead8`
- Sogan: `#593923`
- Deep maroon: `#5d1720`
- Antique gold: `#b18a4f`
- Soft charcoal: `#332923`

## Motif discipline

- Use abstract pendopo roof and gebyok post geometry.
- Keep maroon outside the live text field.
- Do not add full-page batik or literal palace scenery.

## Phase boundary

These files are review previews only. They are not final production covers, transparent gate layers, or reusable content assets. Phase 2 must wait for explicit concept approval.
