# JAWA — Sekar Keraton · Phase 1

Visual revision: `phase-1-concept-r1`. This is review material only and is not an immutable database artwork version.

Three directions are prepared for selection:

1. **Ivory Keraton** — the calmest and most flexible. Ivory paper, hairline sogan/gold framing, one small kayon, and kawung dividers.
2. **Maroon Pendopo** — the most ceremonial and architectural. Deep-maroon framing around an ivory text field with abstract pendopo/gebyok geometry.
3. **Sogan Batik** — the strongest traditional editorial voice. Warm sogan paper with diagonal parang edge rhythm and restrained kawung punctuation.

Each concept includes six 1024×1536 review previews:

- Ornament cover
- Foto Berbingkai
- Berdua
- Terbagi
- Premium gate closed state
- Quiet content section

The concept board also records the palette, typography recommendation, motif discipline, and text-safety intent.

## Shared rules demonstrated

- Invitation hierarchy remains centered in a clear corridor.
- Antique gold is muted, never bright metallic yellow.
- Motifs remain on edges, dividers, or one ceremonial focal point.
- No wayang characters, palace scenery, tourism-poster composition, cross-regional ornament, or bottom floral arrangement.
- Preview typography is mock content. The concept foundations contain no invitation copy.
- No Phase 2–5 production assets have been created.

## Approval checkpoint

Choose one direction, or request a focused hybrid such as “Ivory Keraton structure with the Maroon Pendopo palette.” Production work must not begin until that decision is explicit.
