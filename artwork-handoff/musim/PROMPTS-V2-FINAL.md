# Built-in image editing prompt set — `v2-final`

Each v2 cover was supplied as the edit target. The shared invariant was: preserve the approved composition, palette, paper identity, 2:3 framing, and typography hierarchy; change only decoration entering the live-text envelope; keep production art text-free.

- **Spring Standard:** remove petals and botany from x 15–85%, y 54–78%; lower the floral arrangement below y 79%.
- **Spring Premium:** remove garden scenery; clear x 10–90%, y 54–79%; replace scenery with blush vellum fibers, pearlescent diffuse light, and edge glazing; begin lower botany below y 81%.
- **Summer Standard:** clear x 10–90%, y 54–80%; move right botany below y 82%; preserve quiet sea-glass paper.
- **Summer Premium:** remove all literal horizon, sunset, beach, and ocean scenery; keep two or three abstract ripples below y 81%; add controlled luminous paper depth; move right botany below y 82%.
- **Autumn Standard:** retain no more than two faint detached leaves at the extreme edges; lower the floral arrangement below y 79%.
- **Autumn Premium:** retain at most one faint edge leaf; lower copper twigs below y 80%; increase cotton-paper fibers, warm glazing, and copper edge depth.
- **Winter Standard:** reduce lower botany by roughly 25% and keep it below y 80%; preserve a plain luminous text field.
- **Winter Premium:** remove mountain/landscape silhouettes; replace them with an abstract frost-gray and icy-blue wash; reduce lower botany by roughly 30% and keep it below y 81%; add pearlescent fibers and subtle frost embossing.

All prompts prohibited baked text, UI, watermarks, new large objects, and decoration behind names, guest information, or CTA.
