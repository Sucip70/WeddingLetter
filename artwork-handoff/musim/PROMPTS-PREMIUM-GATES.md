# Final image-generation prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation. Use case: `stylized-concept`.

Four separate 1024×1536 text-free atmospheric bases were generated from the attached direction:

- Spring: warm ivory handmade paper, faint blush light, three to five tiny edge petals, uninterrupted center; no sheet, ribbon, seal, bouquet, garden, scenery, or typography.
- Summer: sea-glass aqua fading into warm ivory, abstract translucent glazing, uninterrupted center; no pearl, waves, reflection, flowers, foliage, beach, island, hills, horizon, sun, surf, or typography.
- Autumn: warm cream handmade paper, restrained cognac corner glazing, at most two tiny edge leaves, uninterrupted center; no card, tracing sheet, central leaf, forest, landscape, canopy, bouquet, or typography.
- Winter: white and icy-blue atmospheric paper-light wash, blue-gray edge depth, tiny frozen-dew points, uninterrupted center; no monogram, glass pane, flowers, mountains, crystals, aurora, castle, snowflakes, landscape, or typography.

Interactive and transition layers were constructed as deterministic full-artboard transparent assets so animation anchors, alignment, and production text separation remain exact.
