## Tier differentiation

- **Standard:** flatter, quieter paper; fewer pigment layers; simpler divider presentation.
- **Premium:** deeper paper grain, controlled luminous glazing, richer edge pigment, and a refined divider with fine flanking rules. Premium does not add another large decorative object.

## Corrections completed

- Spring petals and lower botany were cleared from guest/CTA space; Premium now uses pearlescent blush paper depth with no garden scenery.
- Summer has no literal ocean horizon, sunset, or beach. Premium uses three abstract sea-glass ripples below the CTA; right-side foliage was moved out of the live-text envelope.
- Autumn permanent detached leaves were reduced to no more than two faint edge leaves on Standard and one edge leaf on Premium. Additional leaf motion is reserved for post-open animation.
- Winter mountains were removed. Premium uses an abstract frost-gray/icy-blue wash, and lower botany was reduced and moved beneath the CTA.
