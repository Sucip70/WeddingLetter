# Seasonal v2 image-generation prompt set

Mode: built-in image generation with the v1 cover and page artwork supplied as visual references. All generated production backgrounds are text-free and 2:3 portrait.

## Shared Standard prompt

Refine the theme into elegant editorial watercolor wedding stationery. Preserve an uninterrupted central typography corridor spanning approximately 15–85% of the width and 22–78% of the height. Confine botanical decoration to two diagonal outer corners and a shallow lower edge. Keep contrast behind names, guest information, and CTA uniform and pale. No text, monogram, UI, people, mockup, watermark, full border, or large foreground.

## Shared Premium prompt

Use the approved Standard composition as the safe-field reference. Add cinematic depth only through controlled light, translucent pigment, and a low atmospheric wash. Preserve the same broad typography corridor. Cinematic means tonal depth—not more objects. No separate large foreground, fantasy glow, dense scenery, text, UI, people, mockup, or watermark.

## Theme constraints

- **Spring:** blush sakura, peony, pale sage, ivory; romantic and airy; reduce the bottom bouquet; Premium may add only faint garden haze.
- **Summer:** sea-glass aqua, ivory, teal, restrained champagne; white coastal flowers and thin sea oats; abstract low ripples only; prohibit beach, horizon, island, shells, bubbles, rays, dramatic surf, and tropical-poster density.
- **Autumn:** cream, copper, cognac, rust, dark umber; very few detached maple leaves; warm editorial luxury; prohibit landscape, dense canopy, leaf storm, valley, lake, and oversized flowers.
- **Winter:** white, frost gray, icy blue, navy; fine pine, silver twig, white hellebore; prohibit purple, crystals, aurora, mountains, castles, and fantasy lighting.

## Targeted refinements

- Summer Premium: replace the literal ocean horizon and sunset with a shallow abstract sea-glass wash and two or three translucent ripple strokes below the CTA.
- Autumn Premium: replace the lake, mountains, and forest panorama with a cream-to-amber wash plus two slim copper twigs and three small maple leaves along the bottom edge.
